About the project

The Challenge

SPHEREx maps the whole sky every six months in 102 bands of near-infrared light.

🌌

Over a billion objects

Stars, asteroids, comets, brown dwarfs and galaxies. Many move between observations.

👁️

Comparing over time

Objects like Pluto were discovered by comparing images taken at different times.

👥

Citizen science

A public tool is needed so anyone can explore SPHEREx.

How it works

Search an object

A name or coordinates. We look up where it is and how it moves.

Fetch from IRSA

SPHEREx exposures covering that spot are downloaded and aligned on one sky grid.

See it on the map

It appears in the 3D sky with its colour image and one picture per detector.

Blink it

Flip through the exposures in time, with the motion path and difference view, to spot what moves.
