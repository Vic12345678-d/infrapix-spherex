How to read it: the cyan circle marks your object on each date. With “Follow the object” on, the object stays in the circle and the background stars slide past; with it off, the stars stay put and the circle moves. The cyan line is the object's path across all dates. The right panel shows only what changed: red = brighter than the average frame, blue = fainter, so a mover shows as a red spot next to a blue one.
