Infrared images

What the coloured dots are

  • Blue Hot stars. They shine brightest at the shortest wavelengths (0.75–1.6 µm), with little dust in the way.
  • Green Brightest at 1.6–3.8 µm: usually cool giant stars, or stars seen through some dust that dims their blue light.
  • Red Brightest at 3.8–5 µm: cool, dusty sources, such as young stars still wrapped in their birth cloud, or heavily reddened background stars.

White dots are bright at every wavelength, usually very bright stars.
